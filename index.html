Bio

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at UT San Antonio, advised by Dr. Mitra Bokaei Hosseini in the Data-Driven Software Engineering Lab. My research sits at the intersection of Software Engineering, Requirements Engineering, software evolution, and AI-assisted software engineering.

I use large language models, natural language processing, software repository mining, testing, and empirical methods to recover stakeholder intent, improve requirements quality, evaluate whether software changes satisfy goals, and validate human- and AI-generated software. My research direction focuses on evidence-grounded software assurance, reliable software evolution, and trustworthy AI-assisted software development.
